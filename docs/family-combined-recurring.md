# Family payment grouping

The family payer chooses combined (default) or separate payment from the family payment form. Family members do not get access to the payer's stored checkout capabilities.

- Only READY/new recurring lines with identical start date, end date and number of monthly charges are grouped. All currently supported recurring lines have 12 monthly charges on the first; one-off lines never enter a recurring group. Different periods remain separate. Pure one-off families retain the original aggregate flow unless separate payment or different terms requires a split.
- Amounts and coupon allocations are calculated per line in cents by the server, then summed once. A group containing 500 and 280 with 10% off creates one series for 702, with individual entitlement allocations 450 and 252. Every charge keeps those allocations and the original coupon snapshot.
- The signed order contains `gp`, an immutable list of beneficiary/plan/amount/mode allocations. The provider receives one amount and one shared schedule. The receipt ledger records one total receipt under the payer, not duplicate receipts per person. Entitlements are projected separately to every participant from the verified series event. A decline of that shared series affects its participants, not unrelated plans.
- Ownership, freezes, current-month receipts and pending series are rechecked for EVERY line before dispatch. The CAS reservation rejects overlap with any participant's pending checkout. A submitted group cannot be rewritten by switching radio options. New members never resize an existing instruction: they get a new independent payment; already handled lines stay excluded.
- Shared instructions can be recovered from either participant's manager sync and checked by the daily reconciliation from PR271. The backend permits a shared series ID on other users only when those users/plans are in the same server-owned group order.
- Cancellation requires a second explicit shared-series acknowledgement and applies to all linked plans/users. It does not refund previous charges. A pending-before-first-charge instruction has no fake receipt: any provider-side cancellation not exposed by the existing receipt-based UI must be handled in iCredit and verified by the manager.
- IMPORTANT: freezing a user locally does not change the amount charged by Rivhit. The UI warns payer and staff. Automatic repricing/partial cancellation is NOT implemented and no existing provider instruction is modified. Staff must arrange the change with Rivhit before promising a reduced bill; separate payment avoids coupling if future freezes are expected.
- No existing instructions are merged retroactively. Additive JSON fields only; existing individual series and legacy batches keep their old behavior. No production credentials, live customer updates or real transactions were used during implementation.

Official request contract: https://rivhit-api.readme.io/reference/post_api-paymentpagerequest-svc-geturl (one recurring schedule per payment page). The grouping is application bookkeeping, not a provider-side multi-contract feature.
