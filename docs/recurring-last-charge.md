# Last debit versus membership coverage

Rivhit's reported EndDate is not blindly used as exclusive access expiry. A November 1 setup with 12 monthly debits through October 1 covers membership until November 1 of the following year.

Recovery accepts that interpretation only when the provider end is the first day of the final month of the original 12-month calendar schedule, and the saved checkout and signed order agree on the exclusive coverage end. Identity, merchant, exact discounted amount, currency, active status, monthly cadence, 12-charge count and receipt checks remain mandatory. No extra month is added to arbitrary dates or exact-date legacy contracts.

Manual and bulk recovery and known-series daily reconciliation share this validator. The manual preview distinguishes last debit from coverage end. No provider charge/create/cancel operations or automatic production data migration are introduced.

Abandoned checkout continuation is a separate issue and is not changed here.
